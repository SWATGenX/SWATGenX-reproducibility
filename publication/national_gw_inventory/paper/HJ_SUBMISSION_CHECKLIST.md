# Submission checklist — Hydrogeology Journal (Springer, IAH)

Source: HJ *Instructions for Authors* (Springer, Mar/Apr 2022). Free subscription route confirmed:
**"There are no submission or price-per-page fees, and no fee for use of color."** OnlineOpen (APC
~$3,490) is optional — decline it at the licensing step. Submit via **Editorial Manager**.

## ⚠️ Two blockers / gotchas (read first)

1. **HJ requires Microsoft Word, NOT LaTeX.** Verbatim: *"Do not submit TeX or LaTeX files or other
   formats for text and tables."* Our manuscript is LaTeX. → Keep `main.tex` as the working copy; produce
   a **.docx** for submission (single column, double-spaced, ≥2.5 cm margins, 10-pt Times, continuous line
   + page numbers, tables via Word table function). This is a real conversion task to schedule near submit.
2. **No preprint-with-DOI allowed.** Verbatim: *"a manuscript that has had prior publication as a preprint
   with a DOI may not be submitted to Hydrogeology Journal."* → **Do NOT post the manuscript to arXiv** (this
   reverses my earlier priority-locking suggestion). Protect priority instead via the **Zenodo *data* DOI**
   — a dataset deposit is not a manuscript preprint, so it does not disqualify us. Manuscript stays unposted.

## Drop (these were GSD-specific, HJ does NOT require them)
- Highlights (the 5 bullets) — not an HJ element.
- Graphical abstract — not required by HJ.
- Sustainability/transferability over-framing — retarget to a hydrogeologic-data contribution.

## Not required / favorable
- **Suggested reviewers:** not requested by HJ (the Editor/Associate Editor assign). Optional.
- **Peer review is single-blind** — no manuscript anonymization needed (don't strip author info).
- First decision ~3 months; up to 2 months allowed for revisions.

## Sequence (do-now vs. at-submission)
**Now (content, venue-agnostic-safe):** finish + freeze the data; reference-style swap; trim keywords;
add author-contributions; trim/justify length; regenerate figures at 300 dpi; rewrite cover letter for HJ;
depersonalize prose.
**At submission:** convert `main.tex` → `.docx`; activate the Zenodo **data** DOI and fill it into the
data-availability statement; submit via Editorial Manager; decline OnlineOpen (choose subscription).
**Do NOT:** post the manuscript as an arXiv/preprint with a DOI.

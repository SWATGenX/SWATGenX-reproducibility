Cover letter — Geodata and AI (Elsevier)

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "A national inventory of well lithology and hydraulics for the
conterminous United States, with a vision-language method for reading scanned driller's logs," for
publication as a Research Article in Geodata and AI.

The work sits squarely at the intersection of the journal's two core interests — AI methods for the
geosciences and the compilation and sharing of open geo-databases. It contributes both:

(1) A geo-database. We assemble and openly release a harmonized national inventory of more than 23.9
million depth-resolved lithology intervals across 3.4 million water wells (17 states), plus structured
hydraulics for about 2.8 million wells, drawn entirely from public state-agency endpoints with no privileged
access. To our knowledge this is the first openly-licensed, depth-resolved subsurface-lithology layer
assembled at national scale from primary driller's-log sources; it fills a gap that continental groundwater
models currently approximate with coarse, surface, map-derived geology.

(2) A GeoAI method. We introduce a reproducible, cost-bounded pipeline that uses vision-language models to
recover structured lithology directly from scanned driller's-log images — no OCR stage, no per-form
templates, no per-state training. It is validated against a state agency's own hand-transcriptions (96.0%
coarse/fine accuracy, Cohen's kappa = 0.92) and metered in provider-independent token units (about 1,000 tokens
per log), so the economics are reproducible by any group. We also report an honest, systematically-probed
national availability census of what each state does and does not expose.

We believe this is a strong fit for Geodata and AI specifically because the journal recognizes geo-databases
as a first-class contribution and foregrounds AI for geoscientific discovery — the two halves of this paper.
The frozen dataset and the essential method code are deposited in a public repository (Zenodo) with a citable
DOI (activated on acceptance) and cited in the manuscript; a preprint policy-compliant arrangement.

The work is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and has not been previously published. A
declaration of competing interests (the author maintains the SWATGenX platform that hosts an optional
interactive viewer), a funding statement (none), a CRediT statement, and a declaration of generative-AI use
in manuscript preparation (distinct from the vision-language models used as the research method) are all
included. We would be glad to suggest reviewers with expertise in groundwater data systems and AI-assisted
document extraction upon request.

This manuscript is submitted before 31 August 2026, and we respectfully note the journal's APC (article
processing charge) waiver for submissions received by that date.

\section*{Code and data availability}
The PFAS fate-and-transport engine is implemented as a set of Fortran modules and routines within a SWAT\textsuperscript{+} fork and will be made available on publication. The SWAT\textsuperscript{+} model of the Rogue River watershed is generated by the SWATGenX platform from public national datasets: NHDPlus High Resolution (U.S.\ Geological Survey), 3DEP elevation (USGS), gSSURGO soils (USDA-NRCS), and national land-use and climate layers. Ambient PFOS observations are from the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE) surface-water monitoring program. Streamflow observations are from the USGS National Water Information System (station 04118500). The calibration objective code and the model--observation comparison are provided with the engine repository.

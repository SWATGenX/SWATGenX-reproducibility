\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
  \item A complete PFAS surface-water fate-and-transport engine is implemented in modern SWAT\textsuperscript{+}.
  \item Soil three-phase equilibrium couples to reach-resolved land and in-stream transport.
  \item In-stream routing is cross-validated against the SWAT\textsuperscript{+} channel solver to floating-point precision.
  \item A high-resolution NHDPlus~HR network assigns each PFAS observation to its correct reach.
  \item On the Rogue River the model reproduces the Wolverine-source PFOS downstream gradient.
\end{itemize}

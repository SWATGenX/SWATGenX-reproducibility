\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
We have implemented and verified a complete PFAS surface-water fate-and-transport engine in modern SWAT\textsuperscript{+}, comprising a double-precision soil three-phase equilibrium solver, land-phase mobilization through runoff, lateral flow, leaching, and sediment, a dedicated in-stream routine, mass-conserving reservoir mixing, per-reach point sources, and reach-resolved concentration output. The engine is anchored to two field-independent correctness checks---a $1.7\times10^{-7}$ agreement of the equilibrium solver with a 40-digit reference, and numerical identity of the in-stream routine with the established SWAT\textsuperscript{+} pesticide channel solver on its linear-partition subset to floating-point tolerance---so that the new physics is provably confined to the soil column and the channel mathematics is reused rather than reinvented. Coupled to automated, high-resolution model generation from NHDPlus High Resolution and national environmental data, the engine assigns ambient PFAS observations to their hydrologically correct reaches and, on the Rogue River, reproduces the observed mainstem PFOS gradient toward the Wolverine Worldwide contaminated-site corridor at a log-space root-mean-square error of $0.15$~dex along the source-bearing mainstem ($0.74$~dex over all $20$ gauged reaches) with a single global soil-loading parameter, while the structured residuals---corroborated by a $120$-member uncertainty ensemble that brackets the mainstem but not the tributaries---localize the concentrated point source and per-land-use soil heterogeneity. The result is a transferable, mass-conserving causal layer for PFAS---complementary to monitoring and statistical occurrence models---that supplies the source attribution and counterfactual capability on which the post-2024 regulatory regime depends. Immediate priorities are an explicit, topology-placed representation of the Wolverine source, a class-resolved soil initialization to reduce tributary scatter, extension to additional analytes and to a multi-year temporal evaluation, and application to the post-source-control sediment-legacy regime the engine's benthic compartment is positioned to test.

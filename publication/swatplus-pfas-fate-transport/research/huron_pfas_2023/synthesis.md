## 2. KEY SHIFTS vs the 2023 Paper's World

1. **Federal MCLs are now real (and Huron-binding).** In 2023 there was no enforceable federal drinking-water limit; now **4 ng/L PFOA/PFOS** are final and survived the 2025 partial rollback. Compliance pushed to 2031. The Huron-driving compounds (PFOS, PFOA) stay regulated; the rescission only touches PFHxS/PFNA/GenX/HI [D1].

2. **The "source-control success story" has a sting in the tail.** Water-column control (pre-2023, ~99% drop) held, **but fish-tissue PFOS plateaued above the advisory threshold** — and 2025 science (Kent Lake, Endicott) attributes this to **sediment acting as a secondary internal source** [D3]. This is the single most modeling-relevant change.

3. **The primary source is not actually eliminated.** The 2023 narrative treated Tribar as "controlled." 2025 enforcement shows the **shuttered site still leaks PFOS via stormwater (outfall up to 220,000 ppt)** — i.e., a residual source that now enters via *uncontrolled stormwater* rather than the WWTP pathway [D1,D2,D4].

4. **Tribar's legal/operational status flipped:** criminal conviction (Apr 2025, chromium), facility closed/moved to Howell, and an active civil PFAS suit. Adept Plastic Finishing newly named as co-source [D2].

5. **Advisory science tightened, not relaxed.** PFOS fish trigger **300→50 ppb**; the binding constraint is now fish-tissue, not water ppt. Only the lowest reach (below I-275) was relaxed [D1,D3].

6. **The contaminant story broadened beyond PFOS.** New data show **PFHxA dominant in water** and **FPePA / 6:2 FTS significant in sediment** — the paper's PFOS-only focus is now incomplete [D3].

7. **New machine-readable datasets exist** that didn't in 2023: EGLE PFAS Surface Water ArcGIS feed (per-site ppt), EGLE interactive map, USGS Great Lakes tributaries water+sediment dataset, Ecology Center per-fish/per-congener results [D3].

## 3. RELEVANCE TO OUR WORK

### (a) The SWAT+ PFAS model on this watershed

- **Add a sediment compartment / sediment–pore-water exchange as a first-class state variable.** The Kent Lake plateau means a water-column-only port will mis-predict fish tissue. Sediment is a secondary *source*, not a sink [D3]. This is the highest-priority structural change.
- **Source term is now bimodal in time:** point-source WWTP loading was ~shut off pre-2023, so 2018→present dynamics are **legacy desorption / transient release**, not steady loading — plus a **new diffuse stormwater source from the abandoned Tribar site** (220,000 ppt outfall) that should be a distinct, post-2024 source term [D2,D4]. The model's source forcing changes character across the calibration window.
- **New calibration/validation data:** EGLE ArcGIS surface-water feed (per-site ppt time series), Ecology Center 2023 fish (100 fish/12 species/15 sites, total PFAS 11–133 ppb, PFOS = 58% of Huron content), 2025 Kent Lake fish+sediment+macroinvertebrate set, EGLE Feb-2026 Huron fillet sampling, and the USGS regional dataset (good for *watershed-attribute* parameterization — urban/airport/WWTP-flow drivers — though Huron itself was **not** sampled) [D2,D3].
- **Shift the calibration target** from water-column ppt to **fish-tissue PFOS vs the 50 ppb threshold**, which is the regulatory and ecological binding constraint [D3].
- **Multi-congener support:** at minimum add PFHxA (water) and 6:2 FTS / FPePA (sediment) alongside PFOS/PFOA [D3].
- **A ready-made source-reduction scenario:** simulate the 2018 carbon-treatment step change and test whether sediment legacy alone reproduces the observed fish plateau — this is a clean validation experiment because the source step is well dated and the ~99% water decline is documented.

### (b) Positioning a follow-up paper

- **Strongest publishable hook: modeling the post-source-control plateau.** "Source control worked in the water column but fish stayed above advisory because of sediment legacy" is a quantitative, policy-relevant story that the field is actively debating (Endicott 2025 framed the hypothesis observationally; a calibrated SWAT+ fate-and-transport model could *mechanistically test* it). Differentiates from the 2023 paper's loading-era focus.
- **Management-scenario paper:** with a residual stormwater source (220k ppt outfall) now quantified-by-allegation, simulate scenarios — full site remediation vs stormwater-only control vs sediment dredging/capping — and project fish-tissue recovery time vs the 50 ppb threshold. Directly feeds the AG-suit remedy debate and HRWC advocacy.
- **Multi-congener / emerging-PFAS extension** (PFHxA, FPePA, 6:2 FTS) is novel relative to PFOS-only literature.
- **Caveat for positioning:** much of the dramatic decline (1,400→6 ppt) is *pre-2023* and already reported by EGLE — not new. The novelty must come from the **mechanistic sediment-legacy model and forward scenarios**, not from re-reporting the decline.

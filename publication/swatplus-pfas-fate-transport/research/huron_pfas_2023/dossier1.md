# DOSSIER — PFAS in the HURON RIVER WATERSHED since 2023 (Stream 1: Regulatory & Public-Health Advisories)

Scope: SE Michigan Huron River; Tribar/Wixom source, EGLE/MDHHS advisories, EPA NPDWR impact on Ann Arbor (Huron-sourced intake). Status as of June 2026. CONFIRMED vs PROPOSED flagged throughout.

## DATED TIMELINE

**2018 (baseline, pre-2023):** "Do Not Eat the Foam" surface-water advisory + Do Not Eat Fish advisory issued for the Huron after Tribar Plant 4 (chrome plating, Wixom) was found discharging PFOS to the Wixom sanitary sewer (up to 28,000 ppt), passing through the Wixom WWTP to Norton Creek (max 5,600 ppt) and into the river (max ~1,400 ppt). Industrial PFOS pretreatment installed 2018; Norton Creek fell to 12.2 ppt by Aug 2020 (a 99.8% decline) — this is the long-running historical context, not a post-2023 event.

**2022:** Tribar released ~10,000 gallons of hexavalent-chromium wastewater into the Wixom sewer/Huron system (Aug 2022). New Wixom WWTP discharge permit issued Nov 2022 (criticized by HRWC as inadequate to protect the river from PFAS). [Pre-window, but the direct precursor to the 2025 enforcement.]

**Aug 2023 — CONFIRMED.** City of Ann Arbor updated its **PFAS Action Plan** (the standing utility-response document). Ann Arbor draws ~85% of its drinking water from the Huron River; the utility's self-imposed targets are **<8 ng/L PFOS+PFOA combined** and **<50 ng/L total PFAS** — stricter than both EPA and Michigan limits.

**Apr 10, 2024 — CONFIRMED (federal).** EPA finalized the **PFAS National Primary Drinking Water Regulation (NPDWR)**: enforceable MCLs of **4.0 ng/L (ppt) each for PFOA and PFOS**, plus MCLs for PFHxS, PFNA, HFPO-DA (GenX) and a Hazard Index for mixtures. Original timeline: initial monitoring by 2027, compliance by 2029. *Direct Huron relevance: Ann Arbor's intake is the Huron, so this is the binding standard for Huron-sourced drinking water — though the city already meets it.*

**2024 — CONFIRMED.** Michigan's regional **Eat Safe Fish Guides** continued the partial relaxation: the Do-Not-Eat-Fish advisory was relaxed for the **lowest Huron reach in Wayne County, from I-275 downstream to Lake Erie** (includes the Flat Rock impoundment and Huroc Park) — some species/servings allowed there. **The entire Huron above I-275 remained under Do Not Eat Fish.** Species detail (PFOS-driven): rock bass = Do Not Eat; bluegill/sunfish = max 8 servings/month for a healthy adult.

**Apr 2025 — CONFIRMED (criminal).** Tribar sentenced in connection with the 2022 chromium release: **$200,000 criminal fine + probation.** Same month, Tribar was **sold; the new owner closed the Wixom facilities** and relocated Tribar Technologies operations to **Howell, MI.**

**May 14, 2025 — CONFIRMED (federal, partial rollback).** EPA announced it will **keep** the 4 ng/L PFOA and PFOS MCLs but **extend the compliance deadline from 2029 to 2031**, and proposed to **rescind** the MCLs for the other four PFAS (PFHxS, PFNA, HFPO-DA/GenX, and the Hazard Index). *Huron relevance: the two compounds that drive Huron contamination (PFOS, PFOA) remain federally regulated at 4 ng/L; the rollback does not weaken the Huron-relevant limits.*

**June 2, 2025 — CONFIRMED (state public-health).** MDHHS released the **2025 Eat Safe Fish Guides**. The statewide **Do Not Eat threshold for PFOS in fish was lowered from 300 ppb to 50 ppb** (≈6× more protective), based on updated PFOS toxicity science. Statewide Do-Not-Eat waterbodies jumped from **92 to 188**. This more-protective threshold tightens guidance on the Huron's PFOS-contaminated fish (the more-protective direction, applied to the Huron's existing advisories).

**June 12, 2025 — CONFIRMED (state civil enforcement) — THE HEADLINE NEW EVENT.** Michigan AG **Dana Nessel filed a civil lawsuit** against **Tribar Technologies and Adept Plastic Finishing** (announced June 17, 2025) alleging the companies **continue to discharge PFAS-contaminated stormwater into the Huron River** from the shuttered Wixom properties, in violation of state environmental law and a prior **EGLE consent order**. Key numbers: one **stormwater outfall tested at 220,000 ppt PFAS**; Tribar had received **four EGLE violation notices since 2018**. Relief sought includes stormwater-infrastructure upgrades, monitoring, penalties, and possible halt of Michigan operations. *This is the live, unresolved 2025 matter — the same company, second-time pollution of the Huron.*

**May 18, 2026 — CONFIRMED (federal, proposed rule).** EPA announced a **proposed rule to uphold the PFOA/PFOS 4 ng/L MCLs** while adjusting implementation; comment periods close **July 20, 2026.** (Proposed, not final.)

- Michigan drinking-water MCL: PFOS 16 ng/L, PFOA 8 ng/L (since Aug 2020).
- Ann Arbor finished water: self-target <8 ng/L PFOS+PFOA, <50 ng/L total PFAS — reported to meet all standards; GAC-treated.
- Fish PFOS Do Not Eat threshold: **300 → 50 ppb** (2025 guide); statewide DNE waterbodies 92 → 188.

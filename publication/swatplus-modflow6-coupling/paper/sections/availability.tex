\section*{Code and data availability}

All models, code, and data required to reproduce the results reported here are archived in a public
Zenodo repository (DOI~\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20838389}{10.5281/zenodo.20838389}): the
SWAT\textsuperscript{+} PFAS surface-water model, the on-the-fly MODFLOW~6 groundwater
flow-and-transport model, the GWF--GWT coupling and SFT in-stream routing, the PEST\texttt{++}
pilot-point calibration with the baseflow constraint, the calibrated flow field for the Rogue River
model (USGS~04118500), and the analysis and figure-generating scripts. The SWAT\textsuperscript{+}
engine itself --- including its PFAS fate-and-transport implementation and the MODFLOW~6 coupling ---
is developed openly at \path{https://github.com/rafiei-vahid/swatplus}; its acceleration and
byte-reproducibility methodology are documented separately \citep{rafiei2026engine}.

All inputs are public: USGS streamflow records \citep{data_usgs_nwis} and NHDPlus High Resolution
\citep{data_nhdplus_hr} for delineation, the groundwater-well database used for the hydrogeologic
layers (harmonized at national scale in a companion inventory, \citealp{rafiei2026gwinventory}), and the
EPA ECHO \citep{data_epa_echo} and Michigan EGLE \citep{data_egle_pfas} PFAS source and
observation inventories, alongside the MODFLOW~6 and PEST\texttt{++} executables.

The SWAT\textsuperscript{+} and MODFLOW~6 models analysed here were generated and calibrated with
SWATGenX \citep{Rafiei2026SWATGenX} (\path{https://swatgenx.com}), which automatically builds and
calibrates such models from public data for any U.S.\ Geological Survey gauge in the conterminous
United States. SWATGenX is the
source of the models and the environment in which this study was carried out; the Zenodo archive is
the self-contained package for reproducing the specific results presented here.

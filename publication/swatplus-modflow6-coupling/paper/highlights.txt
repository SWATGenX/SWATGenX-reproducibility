Highlights

- Two-way SWAT+/MODFLOW 6 model carries PFAS across surface water and groundwater
- Validated on 5,383 heads (NSE 0.91), groundwater PFAS, and streambed porewater
- Fingerprinting and joint calibration independently flag legacy groundwater PFAS
- Legacy groundwater discharge is a first-order source of lower-mainstem PFOS
- Global sensitivity analysis maps the surface-versus-groundwater PFAS partition

\subsection{Domain definition and modeling extents}
\label{sec:methods-domain}

The modeling domain is specified by hydrologic units and/or a streamflow outlet consistent with national hydrography. SWATGenX supports multiple \emph{extent modes}---watersheds keyed to USGS streamgages \citep{USGSNWIS}, HUC12 outlet sets, and whole-HUC8 extents---so the same preprocessor is reused across national-scale automation; for any mode, outlet and upstream routing must be unambiguous relative to the NHDPlus HR network after preprocessing (\S\ref{sec:methods-nhd}). Catchment polygons are attributed to WBD HUC8/HUC12 units by spatial join of catchment centroids, carrying downstream HUC12 connectivity (\texttt{tohuc}) where available and deduplicating multipart HUC polygons by largest-area part. Structural reporting uses the S/M/L benchmark domains only (Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-sm-l-products}); the Objective~4 gage basins (\texttt{02297600}, \texttt{05536265}) are separate. For USGS-station workflows, catalog ``basin area'' is the union of exported subbasin polygons for the upstream HUC12 assembly, which can exceed the station drainage area when the gage lies upstream of the HUC12 outlet.

\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item SWATGenX auto-builds executable SWAT\textsuperscript{+} models from NHDPlus HR national datasets.
  \item Every intermediate output is auditable with machine-readable QA sidecars.
  \item A drainage-area audit checks model area against national cumulative drainage.
  \item A $+10$--$17\%$ mainstem offset is localized to QSWAT\textsuperscript{+}/TauDEM, not preprocessing.
  \item Generated models enter standard calibration, verification, and sensitivity workflows.
\end{itemize}

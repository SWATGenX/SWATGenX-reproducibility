\section{NHDPlus HR source resolution and version provenance}
\label{sec:supp-nhdplus-provenance}

NHDPlus HR is a multi-resolution product: its flowlines derive from the National Hydrography Dataset, whose source map scale was contributed by state stewards and therefore varies by region.
The source scale is recorded per source in the \texttt{NHDSourceCitation} table that ships inside each HU4 geodatabase (field \texttt{SourceScaleDenominator}; e.g., 24{,}000 denotes 1:24{,}000), and the per-VPU publication vintage is carried in the distribution package.
Across the conterminous-U.S.\ tiles in our local archive the finest source scale ranges from 1:1{,}200 to 1:24{,}000, and reach density---a proxy for effective network resolution---spans roughly an order of magnitude (about 1.0~reaches\,km\textsuperscript{-2} in the coarsest tiles, such as the lower-Michigan VPUs sourced only at 1:24{,}000, to nearly 18~reaches\,km\textsuperscript{-2} in the densest, which incorporate sub-1:5{,}000 local hydrography).

Because each generated model inherits the source resolution and vintage of its VPU, SWATGenX writes these provenance fields into every model directory (a \texttt{README.md} and a machine-readable \texttt{provenance.json}) at build time; the per-VPU publication vintage is recovered from the geodatabase's own ESRI metadata (the maximum \texttt{CreaDate}), and an archive-wide registry is maintained per VPU.
Table~\ref{tab:nhdplus-provenance} reports the hydrography provenance for the eight catalog Model IDs.
The Illinois calibration basin (VPU~\texttt{0712}) draws on the finest local sources (to 1:1{,}200) and the densest network; the two semi-arid Arizona benchmarks (VPUs~\texttt{1505}, \texttt{1506}) are sourced at 1:24{,}000.
This heterogeneity bounds how finely a basin's channels---and therefore the number of gaged reaches available for calibration---can be resolved, independent of the SWAT\textsuperscript{+} assembly.

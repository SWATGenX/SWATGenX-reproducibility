\section*{Data Availability Statement}

\begin{sloppypar}
SWATGenX preprocessing documentation, manuscript analysis scripts, derived evaluation tables, and figure-generation workflows are archived in the public reproducibility repository \citep{RafieiSWATGenXrepro}, \url{https://github.com/SWATGenX/SWATGenX-reproducibility}, where the release tag used for peer review is recorded so that readers can retrieve a frozen copy of the workflow described here.
The executable SWAT\textsuperscript{+} model packages for the evaluation Model IDs (Table~\ref{tab:model-roster}) are available for direct download from the SWATGenX example-models portal (\url{https://www.swatgenx.com/example-models}), with per-model case-study pages for the benchmark domains (for example, the Peace River HUC-8 model at \url{https://www.swatgenx.com/swat-plus-modeling/florida/03100101}).

The archived release includes the model roster, NHDPlus HR preprocessing QA summaries, structural model-complexity tables, drainage-area audit tables and station-assignment inventories, calibration/verification metrics, Morris sensitivity outputs, runtime benchmark tables, and the scripts required to reproduce the manuscript figures and tables. Supplementary tables, figures, and the reproducibility manifest (Supplementary Table~S\ref{tab:repro-file-manifest}, Section~S\ref{sec:supp-repro-manifest}) are provided in a separate \textbf{Supplementary Material} PDF (\texttt{supplement.pdf}).

This study relies on publicly distributed U.S.\ national datasets cited where used---NHDPlus HR hydrography, the Watershed Boundary Dataset, PRISM, NSRDB, NLCD, SSURGO/gSSURGO, and USGS NWIS; downstream users should obtain these from the responsible agencies and note access dates or product versions. The manuscript does not assert open access to private operational servers, internal user workspaces, or credentials.
\end{sloppypar}

\section*{Data Availability Statement}

\begin{sloppypar}
SWATGenX preprocessing documentation, manuscript analysis scripts, derived evaluation tables, and figure-generation workflows are archived in the public reproducibility repository \url{https://github.com/SWATGenX/SWATGenX-reproducibility}, where the release tag used for peer review is recorded so that readers can retrieve a frozen copy of the workflow described here.
The executable SWAT\textsuperscript{+} model packages for the evaluation Model IDs (Table~\ref{tab:model-roster}) are available for direct download from the SWATGenX example-models portal (\url{https://www.swatgenx.com/example-models}), with per-model case-study pages for the benchmark domains (for example, the Peace River HUC-8 model at \url{https://www.swatgenx.com/swat-plus-modeling/florida/03100101}).

The archived release will include the model roster, NHDPlus HR preprocessing QA summaries, structural model-complexity tables, drainage-area fidelity audit tables and station-assignment inventories, calibration and verification metrics, Morris sensitivity outputs, runtime benchmark tables, and scripts required to reproduce the manuscript figures and tables.
Supplementary tables, figures, and the reproducibility manifest are provided in a separate \textbf{Supplementary Material} PDF (\texttt{supplement.pdf}) compiled from \texttt{supplement.tex}.
A file-level manifest is provided as Supplementary Table~S\ref{tab:repro-file-manifest} (see the separate Supplementary Material PDF, Section~S\ref{sec:supp-repro-manifest}).

This study relies on publicly distributed United States national datasets that are cited where used in the manuscript.
They include NHDPlus High Resolution hydrography, the Watershed Boundary Dataset (WBD), PRISM climate grids, the National Solar Radiation Database (NSRDB), the National Land Cover Database (NLCD), SSURGO/gSSURGO soils products, and USGS National Water Information System (NWIS) catalogs for streamgage context.
Downstream users should obtain those products directly from the responsible agencies and note access dates or product versions required by each distributor.

The manuscript does not assert open access to private operational servers, internal user workspaces, or credentials.
Locally generated draft figure products and provisional runtime or inventory timestamps are not treated as archival scientific datasets for this submission.
Any public release of runnable workflow outputs will follow repository policy and the archival record chosen at submission time.
\end{sloppypar}
